How tracking works

Tap 2PT, 3PT or FT, then Make or Miss. For misses, pick a miss type or Skip. An outcome counts as exactly one attempt. Add optional reaction or play note to the last action or use Edit in the log. Touches are recorded catches only (a lower bound). Every action is saved locally and exported with notes and miss types. This is a lightweight tracking aid, not official scorekeeping.
