Enable Goals family sync

This small update allows Goals, progress updates and priority order to sync with your existing family. Existing games, training, videos, memberships and invitation codes are preserved.

  1. Tap Copy setup SQL.
  2. Open your existing GameTrace project in Supabase → SQL Editor → New query. Paste and Run.
  3. After “Success. No rows returned,” refresh GameTrace on each family device and use Family Cloud → Sync now.

Until this update is installed, Goals stay saved on this device. Games and training keep syncing. Do not rerun the original family setup or reset query.
