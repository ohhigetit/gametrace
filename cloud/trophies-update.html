Enable Family Trophies family sync

This small update allows Owner/Admin-created trophies and awards, plus Next Steps progress and priorities to sync with your existing family. Existing games, training, videos, memberships and invitation codes are preserved.

  1. Tap Copy setup SQL.
  2. Open your existing GameTrace project in Supabase → SQL Editor → New query. Paste and Run.
  3. After “Success. No rows returned,” refresh GameTrace on each family device and use Family Cloud → Sync now.

Until this update is installed, Custom family trophies stay disabled. Existing Next Steps stay saved on this device. Games and training keep syncing. Do not rerun the original family setup or reset query.
